MOXIE's Analysis

Remarketing quietly became the standout channel for Dapper Can in August. On a budget of under a hundred dollars it delivered 44,098 impressions and 56 clicks, bringing prior site visitors back for another look at a cost per return that no prospecting channel can match. Geofencing did the wider job, running 33,385 impressions across the target locations and keeping the brand in front of people while they were physically near the places that matter. Month three is when a program stops being an experiment and starts producing readable signal, and the signal here is that the retargeting layer is working harder per dollar than anything else in the mix. That is a useful thing to know heading into a period where the business is weighing what to carry forward. When the question becomes what to keep, the honest answer is the piece already earning its keep, and this month made clear which piece that is.
