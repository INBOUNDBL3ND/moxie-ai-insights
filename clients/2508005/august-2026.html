MOXIE's Analysis

Search continued to pull its weight for Lift Station Depot in August, returning 493 clicks from 10,900 impressions at a 4.53% click-through rate against the 3.17% platform benchmark. That gap matters in a category where the buyer is a contractor or a municipal operations lead who knows exactly what they need, because it says the ads are matching real specification language rather than casting wide. The campaign stayed disciplined about what it promoted, keeping Barmesa and Atlantis live while holding Franklin back until the product is properly represented on the site, which protects the click from landing somewhere that cannot answer the question. SEO ran underneath to keep the product and application pages aligned with how those searches are actually phrased. Late in the month the client asked to sit down and talk through the wider marketing picture, which is the conversation you want after a stretch of steady, above-benchmark performance rather than before it. September opens with that meeting on the calendar and a search program worth building on.
