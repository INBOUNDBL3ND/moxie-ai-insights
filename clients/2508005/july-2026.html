MOXIE's Analysis

Mid-summer is when steady programs quietly earn their keep, and July kept Lift Station Depot moving on that dependable line. Google Search carried the month, putting the brand in front of the buyers typing in exactly the equipment they need at the moment they need it, and its 3.19 percent click-through rate edged past the 3.17 percent platform benchmark, a clean sign the spend is reaching genuine intent. The Barmesa and Atlantis campaigns ran through the month, while the Franklin line stays queued until its pages are live on the website, a deliberate choice to point paid traffic only at destinations ready to convert. SEO ran alongside the ads, building the organic foundation that makes every paid click a little more efficient over the long buying cycle these products carry. Neither side was asked to carry the month alone; the value sits in the way search intent and organic visibility reinforce each other for procurement teams that compare carefully before they commit. That patience is the entire strategy, and it is the part that pays off when a buyer finally comes looking. July set the table for a second half built on the same rhythm.
