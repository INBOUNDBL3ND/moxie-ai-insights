MOXIE's Analysis

The throughline of June was a team in motion: NWO is growing and reorganizing, and much of the month's work went into helping the company put its best face forward as it does. The headline was welcoming Amy on board as Director of Strategic Partnerships, with the team shaping a social announcement built around her background and NWO's growth story and pushing it out across every platform. Behind that, a wave of title changes meant new business cards for several team members and a fresh hire, plus updated letterhead carrying the new office address, the unglamorous brand-consistency work that keeps a scaling company looking buttoned-up. The team also opened a new outbound front, building a direct-mail flyer aimed at personal injury law firms within a hundred miles of St. Louis and starting a joint piece with a local e-waste recycler to share audiences. Organic social kept the brand visible to the wider community through it all, and the website stayed under active management in the background. This is relationship-and-reputation marketing in its most practical form, and June ends with NWO's growing team better equipped to introduce itself to the partners and prospects that come next.
