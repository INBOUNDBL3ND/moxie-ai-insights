MOXIE's Analysis

A new brand needed a new front door, and August built one. Work started on the NWO Technology Advisory website with a mockup drawn from the discovery meeting and the MOXIE document, then a working homepage draft went up with anchor navigation for review. In parallel a custom brand video moved through four rounds to a final approved master with the review watermark stripped, ready to sit above the fold once the inner pages are finished. The MOXIE document itself came back from the client with detailed edits, which is exactly the kind of engagement that makes the finished positioning worth something. Design work ran alongside: two T-shirt concepts for the Needy Paws golf tournament with Fairway Automotive, laid out so the co-sponsor logos have room without crowding the two lead brands. A save-the-date post for the November holiday party went out, and a site health check late in the month confirmed everything current with no alerts. The site recorded 516 sessions. A rebrand is a lot of pieces, and this month most of them came together.
