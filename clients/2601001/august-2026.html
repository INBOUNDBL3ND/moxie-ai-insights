MOXIE's Analysis

The Chamber asked a direct question in August and got a direct answer, then a plan. Lori raised low engagement on Instagram and wanted to know the strategy behind it, so the team pulled the numbers and brought them to a working session. The audience turned out to be genuinely strong: 1,493 followers, eighty-eight percent based in the United States, 518 across the eight core cities, core age thirty-five to sixty-four, and 611 women, which is directly relevant to the RISE conference. Reels were the clear finding, with a recent one drawing 7.8 times the reach of an average static post. Out of that came a shift toward candid, story-led content and a weekly member spotlight framed as a benefit rather than a request. A vertical reel from the men's leadership group was produced and delivered, ribbon-cutting and membership meeting posts went out, and a separate audit found newsletter resends to non-openers had stopped and put them back on a permanent schedule. Organic reach hit 156,811 views. A hard question produced a better program.
