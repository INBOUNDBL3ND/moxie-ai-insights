MOXIE's Analysis

April was an event-marketing month at its core, with two campaigns running side by side as different halves of the same push. The "Vendors Campaign" and the "Event Attendees Campaign" on Facebook Ads aren't competing, they're doing different jobs: one is bringing the people who set up the booths and underwrite the experience, the other is bringing the audience that walks through the door. The vendor side showed strong early traction this month, which lines up with how event signup actually works in real life. Vendors decide weeks before attendees do, because they need lead time to plan their presence, so seeing the vendor campaign accelerate now is a healthy signal that the funnel is filling on the right schedule. Both campaigns will stay active through the lead-up, with the team watching how each curve shapes up as the event approaches. Behind the scenes, the team kept the RISE Women Award Google Form moving so it'll be ready to launch when the timing is right. Going into May, the focus is to keep both halves of the event push working in parallel and start activating attendee-side messaging as the date gets closer.
