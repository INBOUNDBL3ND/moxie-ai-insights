MOXIE's Analysis

When two campaigns share a budget, the audience always picks a winner. April made that pick crystal clear. The "Vendors Campaign" came out ahead of the "Event Attendees Campaign" on Facebook Ads this month, which is exactly the kind of read we want parallel creative to give us. The May plan is to widen the winning angle while preserving enough of the alternative to keep learning. On top of the campaign comparison, the overall ad reach widened this month, which matters because every additional person who sees the brand now is one less stranger when the next ad lands in front of them. And the engagement followed the impressions, with the clicks landing in real territory this month. Clicks are the part that matter most, because clicks mean the message actually moved someone to act, not just scroll past. That's the signal that creative and audience are matched up the way they should be. On the project front, the RISE Women Award Google Form is teed up and ready to break ground in the coming weeks, with prep work already underway. While the campaign comparison was the headline read, the supporting marketing layer ran on rails, which is what lets the team focus optimizations where they pay off most. Going into May, the plan is to widen the budget on what's working and let the data keep refining the rest.
