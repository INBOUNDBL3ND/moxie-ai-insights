MOXIE's Analysis

A chamber runs on turnout and connection, and July's marketing pointed both at one clear goal: filling the RISE conference with the right people. The centerpiece was a dedicated email blast built for attendees and sponsors, designed to drive award nominations and get the event in front of the members most likely to take part. Around it, two freshly updated reels went out with engagement-prompt wording that invites members to comment, share, and tag, the kind of organic social that turns a passive feed into a conversation. Steady social posting kept the wider membership informed on events and news, doing the quieter work of turning awareness into the renewals and attendance a chamber depends on. The strength of this program is how the pieces reinforce one another: email carries the direct ask, while social keeps the relationship warm between sends. Into the rest of the year, the focus stays on pairing consistent visibility with the personal touch that makes members feel like they belong.
