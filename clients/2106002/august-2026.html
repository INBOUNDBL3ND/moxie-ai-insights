MOXIE's Analysis

The head-to-head test on TikTok produced a clear answer this month, and it was a good one for both sides. The AI-generated user content ran to 64,694 impressions and returned 1,695 clicks at a 2.00% click-through rate, while the human-created version reached 84,952 impressions and 1,148 clicks at 1.77%. The platform benchmark sits at 0.84%, so both formats more than doubled it. The AI set won on efficiency and the human set won on reach, which means the useful takeaway is not that one beat the other but that Brown and Brown now has two proven creative engines rather than one. Organic social carried the same energy underneath, drawing 9,835 likes and 1,439 video views across the month and keeping the brand familiar to people between the ads. Running the test properly, with matched budgets and a clean read, is what turned a hunch into evidence. September starts with real data about which creative style to lean on for which job, and that is worth far more than a single strong month.
