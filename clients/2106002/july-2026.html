MOXIE's Analysis

July marked a real step forward for Brown and Brown: the brand moved into paid TikTok, and the debut landed with authority. The team ran two creative angles of the same effort, one built on AI-generated UGC and one on human UGC, so the message could reach shoppers from two directions while pointing at a single goal. Both cleared the bar. The AI UGC ads pulled a 1.29% click-through and the human UGC ads a 1.66%, each running past TikTok's 0.84% platform benchmark, and together they delivered nearly 200,000 impressions and more than 3,000 clicks from people scrolling their feeds. That is a strong first month on a channel where attention is hard-won. Away from the ads, the team deepened the brand's footing in search and AI answers, publishing five SEO and AEO blog posts aimed at the personal-injury questions families ask most, with more queued, and running live ChatGPT and Google AI Overview tests to see how the firm stacks up against competitors. The site's llms.txt file was reviewed and flagged for enhancement so AI engines read the brand cleanly. July opened a powerful new front and sharpened the foundation beneath it at the same time.
