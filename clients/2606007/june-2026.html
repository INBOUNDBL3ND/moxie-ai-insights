MOXIE's Analysis

Some projects are pure fun to build, and End Zone Eddie is one of them. June was about getting to know the product and the story behind it: a wood-base, spinning-frame game-day keepsake with a Happy Eddie and an Ugly Eddie, built around the ritual of turning him after every win or loss. Terri brought the team in to create an explainer video first, with social content to follow later in the year, and she shared a rich set of background materials and a rhyming script outline to work from. The team set up the dashboard and Dropbox for logos and photos, locked the brand colors, and by month-end Lisa had delivered a first brand kit anchored on the team-neutral look the product calls for. The pieces are in place to start animating the myth of Eddie, and the fans who eventually meet him are in for something genuinely playful and full of personality.
