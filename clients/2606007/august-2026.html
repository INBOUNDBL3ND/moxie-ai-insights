MOXIE's Analysis

The explainer video was finished and approved in August, and finishing it well took real persistence. The animation kept reinterpreting the Win Wall from scene to scene, changing the number and arrangement of holes, which is a genuine problem when the video is selling a physical product someone will receive. Rather than accept it, the team supplied reference imagery, specified exactly what was acceptable, a single consistent row every time the product appears, and pushed for the turnaround needed to land it before football season. It was delivered on the tenth. Attention then moved to the month's second piece: ten branded social posts built from the outline the client provided, produced in the End Zone Eddie style, reviewed internally for the small artifacts that creep into generated imagery, revised, and delivered through both the shared folder and the client portal. Two deliverables, both landing in time for the season that matters.
