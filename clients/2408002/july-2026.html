MOXIE's Analysis

July closed a chapter and opened a better-fit one for Organized to Thrive. Through the month, Facebook delivered broad awareness, putting the brand in front of the local community across 295,212 impressions and reaching nearly 18,000 people, with more than 1,100 of them clicking through to learn more. After reviewing where the leads were actually coming from, Della and the team made a deliberate call at a July 28 meeting to pause Facebook and redirect that budget toward a channel better matched to how busy households look for a home organizer. That same meeting mapped a full set of website edits, sharpening the message around compassionate downsizing, giving the About page a personal voice, and adding a clear free-consultation call to action so visitors know exactly how to take the next step. The team also fixed the Facebook link in the site footer and laid out the plan to move toward Google Ads or remarketing for the next phase. This is a service built on trust, and the shift is about meeting people at the moment they are already searching for help. July set the table for a sharper, more intent-driven push into the rest of the year.
