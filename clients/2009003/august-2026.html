MOXIE's Analysis

August turned a recurring headache into a permanent asset. Med-Search needed somewhere to put its open roles, and rather than default to the expensive platform used previously, the team priced the options honestly, flagged that the incumbent had climbed to well over two hundred dollars a month, and recommended a two hundred dollar cheaper alternative that covered everything actually needed. Then it got built: branded to match the updated site, logo, colors and typography in place, custom icons for each position, applications routed to the correct recruiter, public posting and sharing switched off. Six openings went live for review on the twentieth. By the twenty-sixth the board carried all twenty current roles, imported, published individually and verified live. Alongside it, job posts were written for Territory Sales and Surgical Sales territories, then revised twice as the team refined requirements and recruiter assignments. Website management and SEO ran underneath, with the site recording 1,276 sessions. The firm ended August with a candidate destination it owns rather than one it rents.
