MOXIE's Analysis

Foundation work set the tone in July, with the recruiting program's search and analytics groundwork moved firmly into place. The team completed the Rank Math PRO setup, migrated the old configuration cleanly, connected GA4 and Search Console, and uploaded a custom llms.txt so the site is legible to both traditional search and the AI answer engines hiring teams increasingly lean on. A new brand kit followed, logos, colors, and icons all tuned to match the refreshed site, giving every future asset one consistent look. Organic LinkedIn stayed active too, with a refreshed cover image and the start of additional job-ad posters made to catch strong candidates as they scroll. Keyword research and per-page mapping are queued next, the work that turns a solid foundation into steady ranking gains. With 805 sessions on the month, the site is already drawing the operations leaders and hiring managers the program is built to reach, and August picks up with the pieces aligned.
