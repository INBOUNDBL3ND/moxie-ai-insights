MOXIE's Analysis

June deepened the partnership with Bill, with the team turning the candidate and employer resource library from a rough idea into working pages. Most of the month went into the structure that makes that library actually convert, separating the freely accessible tier-one materials from the gated tier-two content, correcting the links so employers and candidates each land in the right place, and building the forms that capture interest along the way. The team also designed and delivered the one-pager, refreshed the tier-one PDFs with cleaner formatting and on-brand color, and added a thank-you landing page so every applicant flows through a measured path. To close the month, the team installed Rank Math Pro, stood up a fresh analytics property, and confirmed traffic was being recorded so the site's search visibility can be watched and improved over time. With analytics now reading every visit, the next round of refinements will have real data to lean on instead of guesswork, which is exactly where a recruiting site should be heading.
