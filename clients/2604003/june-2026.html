MOXIE's Analysis

The partnership with Adrian sharpened its focus in June, aligning the ads with the landing page built to convert them. As soon as Adrian finished the dedicated manufacturing landing page, with its sharpened CIO-focused hero and a new demo-center section on the home page, the team pointed the LinkedIn ads straight at it, so the click and the message now tell one continuous story. The team also confirmed the LinkedIn Insight Tag was firing correctly across the whole site, not just the home page, which keeps the audience data clean as delivery matures past its learning phase. Toward the end of the month the focus widened to content, with a plan to gather every social asset into a shared Dropbox library Adrian can pull from for email and other channels, plus a posting cadence that blends the team's promotional pieces with the educational ones he wants to add each week. With a content library taking shape, the program is ready to speak to decision-makers in more than one voice heading into the back half of the year.
