MOXIE's Analysis

Reach was the headline for U Store It in August. Facebook put the facility in front of 5,488 people across O'Fallon and returned 174 clicks from 38,687 impressions, keeping the units visible to anyone in the area thinking about a move, a renovation or a seasonal clear-out. The digital billboard added a different kind of presence entirely, running 17,800 impressions and 3,080 blips on one board, catching drivers on the routes that pass the property every day. Organic social carried 38,325 video views on the month, which is a substantial amount of local attention for a storage brand. Website management and SEO held the foundation steady underneath it, and the site logged 212 sessions from people far enough along to look up rates and availability. The mix works because each piece meets people in a different place: the board reaches them while they are driving past, Facebook reaches them at home, and search catches them once they have decided. August kept all three running in step.
