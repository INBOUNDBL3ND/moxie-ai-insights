MOXIE's Analysis

Efficiency defined the month for The Tile and Grout Project. Search turned 2,040 impressions into 101 clicks, a 4.95% click-through rate that runs well past the 3.17% platform benchmark, on a budget deliberately kept tight. In a service category where a homeowner searches once, compares two or three names and books, that ratio is the number that matters most. It means the ads are surfacing for people describing an actual problem, grout that has gone dark, tile that will not come clean, rather than for anyone idly researching. A hundred qualified clicks against a modest monthly spend is the kind of arithmetic that makes a specialist trade work, because each one arrives already convinced the job needs doing and only shopping for who does it. The program did not need reinvention this month; it needed to hold its discipline, and it did. That steadiness is what compounds into a full schedule.
