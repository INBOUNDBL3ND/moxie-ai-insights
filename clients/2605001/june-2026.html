Standing up a new in-home care program means putting a lot of pieces in motion at once, and June carried most of that weight. The team took over the existing Google Ads account, rebuilt the campaigns around a dementia and memory-care focus, and the new search ads went live mid-month and moved into Google's learning phase. A Facebook ad account was created and readied for creative drawn from the best-performing social content. Alongside the advertising, the team shipped a full set of social posts and reels, optimized the Google Business Profile, stood up GA4 so website traffic is finally visible, and built a conversion-focused dementia landing page mockup pointed at the free in-home assessment. Brand and print pieces moved in parallel. For families weighing care options across St. Louis, June set a foundation that the months ahead can build real momentum on.
