MOXIE's Analysis

July brought a genuine step-change for Midalloy, headlined by the launch of a new LinkedIn matched-audience campaign. The team cleaned and deduped the contact data, mapped 11,363 US contacts into a custom audience, and set five ads live, and the early read is strong: LinkedIn returned a 3.33 percent click-through rate against a 0.62 percent platform benchmark, while Google Search delivered an 11.84 percent click-through rate versus the 3.17 percent benchmark. Both numbers point to the same thing, a program reaching buyers who already know what they need rather than casual browsers. Around the paid work, the team rebranded the core sales collateral to the new MSM identity, built and published a Surplus Inventory Report page, refreshed the homepage certification logos, and prepared Q2 materials ahead of the July 17 board meeting. The quarter's RFQ tracking told the real story, with 31 quality RFQs qualified, the kind of pipeline signal that matters more than any single metric. With a sharper LinkedIn audience now live and the collateral aligned to the new brand, the program heads into the back half of the year built to turn precision into orders.
