MOXIE's Analysis

This was the month Midalloy's program proved itself to a new audience and a new stakeholder at the same time. LinkedIn returned a 3.20% click-through rate against a 0.62% platform benchmark, more than five times the line, and Google Search reached 16.16% against a 3.17% benchmark on a serious budget, which is the kind of pairing that only happens when the targeting and the specification language are both right. When the private equity group brought in a new marketing lead, the team gave her full access to analytics, Search Console, the ad accounts and the LinkedIn page within days, ran a forty-eight minute working session, and then produced a custom ten-minute briefing video walking through a year of partnership, everything delivered, the results, and exactly how each campaign is structured. Alongside that, the accessibility widget and consent platform went onto the site, the line card logo was corrected, the surplus inventory list was refreshed and a broken line card download was fixed. Earning a new stakeholder's confidence in a fortnight is its own kind of result.
