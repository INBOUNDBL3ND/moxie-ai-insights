MOXIE's Analysis

The clearest signal in June came off the search campaign, where roughly 11,800 impressions produced 871 clicks at a 7.4 percent click rate, a sign the studio is reaching people already looking for a place to dance. The paid search program did the heavy lifting on demand all month, keeping the studio in front of those people right when they start looking. Those clicks fed a website the team kept current through the month, rolling the studio's new phone number across the site and listings at Sterling's request so every place a dancer might call shows the right line. When the page someone lands on matches the promise of the ad that sent them, every dollar of search budget works a little harder, and that consistency is exactly what June was about. The studio heads into summer with a clean, current site and a search engine that is clearly finding the right dancers. The focus now is converting more of that traffic into booked classes.
