MOXIE's Analysis

Search carried Just Dancing through August with real conviction. The campaign turned 13,800 impressions into 888 clicks, a 6.45% click-through rate that doubles the 3.17% platform benchmark, which tells you the ads are landing in front of parents and adults who are already looking for a studio rather than merely browsing. That intent showed up downstream too, with the site recording 1,556 sessions on the month, the busiest of any channel in the account. SEO and website management kept the class schedules, program pages and enrollment paths current underneath, so the people arriving from search found what they came for without hunting. Timing helps here: August is when families settle the fall schedule, and the program was positioned to catch that decision window rather than arrive after it closed. The result is a month where the advertising, the site and the calendar were all pointed at the same moment. That alignment is what turns a seasonal spike into a full studio.
