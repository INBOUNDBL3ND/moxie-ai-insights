MOXIE's Analysis

The clearest signal in July came straight off search, where the studio kept meeting dancers at the exact moment they went looking. Google Search turned 13,200 impressions into 857 clicks at a 6.51% click-through, comfortably ahead of the 3.17% platform benchmark and a sign the campaign is reaching families ready to find a class. That steady stream of high-intent visitors kept flowing to a site the team maintained through the month. Much of July, though, went into what comes next. The team built a set of Facebook ad mockups drawn from the studio's own customer avatars, then sat down for an ad-strategy session and mapped CTA options tied to class pricing and timing so the message lands with the right offer. By month's end the client's ad budget was in hand, clearing the runway to scale the next flight of creative. That groundwork means search will soon have a social partner reaching parents before they even start searching. July kept the proven channel producing while laying the pieces for a wider reach, and the studio heads into the back half of summer with real momentum and a plan to match.
