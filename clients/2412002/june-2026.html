MOXIE's Analysis

June's numbers show a paid program operating at real scale and, more importantly, with real efficiency. Microsoft Ads alone delivered more than 318,000 impressions and over 3,300 clicks, while the remarketing campaign converted at a remarkable 20.3 percent click rate, the clearest sign that buyers who have already seen the brand come back ready to engage. Across every channel the account drew nearly 6,000 clicks and fed almost 11,000 website sessions, a strong month of qualified attention for a supplier whose sales cycle rewards staying visible. P-Max and Shopping kept the catalog in front of people actively sourcing packaging, search held the high-intent middle, and remarketing closed the loop on anyone who did not convert the first time. The mix is doing what a mature B2B program should: meeting buyers at every stage from first discovery through final RFP, so the brand is never out of sight when a purchasing decision firms up. Social posts kept the organic presence current alongside it. June was one of the account's healthiest months on both volume and quality, and the path forward is to keep that remarketing engine fed and protect the efficiency the data is showing.
