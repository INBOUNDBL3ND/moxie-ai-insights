MOXIE's Analysis

Two long projects moved a long way for VisiPak in August. The SEO and AEO pass crossed the halfway mark, with roughly 150 of the site's 260 pages now reviewed and about 65 worked through in a single push, writing titles and descriptions for product pages, case studies, market pages and utility pages with every claim checked against live content first. The golf ball tubes and clamshell packaging pages now score 88 out of 100, with around twenty more sitting in the 81 to 88 range. Separately, the blog author display and author archive were removed site-wide, closing a 404 path that visitors were reaching by clicking a byline. On the advertising side, Google Search returned a 5.08% click-through rate against the 3.17% benchmark and remarketing reached 8.29%, both well clear, while Microsoft Ads delivered 3,778 clicks from 334,951 impressions and Performance Max added another 1,030. The site closed at 577 sessions. Steady technical work and disciplined spend are compounding on each other here rather than running in parallel.
