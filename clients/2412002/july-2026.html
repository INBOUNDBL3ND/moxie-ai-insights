MOXIE's Analysis

Scale and precision rarely travel together, but July's paid program managed both across a five-channel mix. Microsoft Ads alone delivered nearly 396,000 impressions and more than 3,800 clicks, while Google Remarketing converted at a 15.40 percent click-through rate, far beyond the 3.17 percent benchmark and the clearest signal that buyers who already know the brand return ready to engage. Google Search held the high-intent middle with a 6.80 percent click-through rate, also well above that benchmark, proof the account is reaching people actively sourcing packaging. Shopping and Performance Max kept the catalog visible to buyers comparing options, so the funnel stayed covered from first discovery through final RFP. On the technical side, the team installed Rank Math SEO Pro and worked through titles, meta descriptions, and focus keywords across product, case-study, and utility pages, delivering a seventeen-page report scoring 81 to 88. AEO content sections went in to improve AI and ChatGPT citability, AI Max was activated across the Search campaigns, and a floating VisiLock button was added with a reordered Products menu. July was one of the account's healthiest months on both volume and quality, and the path forward is protecting that efficiency while keeping the remarketing engine fed.
