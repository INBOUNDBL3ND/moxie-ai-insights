MOXIE's Analysis

Two very different jobs came together well for Cube Storage this month. Search worked its usual precision, converting 1,010 impressions into 127 clicks at a 12.57% click-through rate, which is four times the 3.17% platform benchmark and about as sharp as local intent capture gets. Facebook covered the wider Paducah area, reaching 11,282 people and returning 564 clicks at 2.08% against the 0.90% benchmark, while geofencing kept the facility present around the neighborhoods that feed it. Away from the media, the team cleaned up the new facility photography, retouching the pavement so the buildings present the way they actually look on a good day, and then built two versions of an 8.5 by 11 tenant flyer for a partner housing complex: a full-color version for commercial printing and a low-ink version that reproduces cleanly on an office printer, both leading with the dollar first month offer. Print and digital rarely get to work the same offer at the same time. This month they did.
