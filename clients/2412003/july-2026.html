MOXIE's Analysis

July was a modernization month for IndePak, aimed squarely at how the brand shows up as search itself changes. The team activated the AI Max optimization layer on the Thermoforming and Trays and Packaging Google Search campaigns, making them eligible for AI Overview placements where buyers increasingly begin their research. To feed those conversational, long-tail queries, core keywords were aligned to broad match so the campaigns can surface for the way specifiers actually phrase a problem. The team also confirmed the Microsoft Ads campaigns are auto-eligible for Copilot placements, extending that same forward posture across a second search audience many competitors overlook. Across the two channels the account drew more than 34,000 impressions and close to 900 clicks from the procurement and engineering teams researching packaging solutions. What makes a B2B program like this compound is staying visible as the rules shift: each channel earns a little trust, the account stays in front of the right teams, and pipeline quality improves quietly month over month. July positioned the program to keep pace with how buyers will search through the back half of the year.
