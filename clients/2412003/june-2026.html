MOXIE's Analysis

June carried IndePak's program forward on the same disciplined footing it has held all spring, with the paid channels doing steady, measurable work against a clearly defined audience. Google Search stayed pointed at the specifiers and buyers actively researching packaging solutions, turning real intent into qualified visits, while Microsoft Ads extended that reach across a second search audience many competitors overlook. Social posting kept the brand visible to the procurement and operations leaders who follow the category between active projects. They also confirmed GA4 is tracking leads correctly off the thank-you page. Each of those makes the destination more credible and better instrumented the moment a click arrives. What makes a B2B program like this compound is consistency: each channel earns a little trust, the account stays in front of the right teams, and pipeline quality quietly improves month over month. June kept that momentum exactly where it needs to be heading into the back half of the year.
