MOXIE's Analysis

Search kept working hard for IndePak in August, and the technical foundation underneath it got noticeably cleaner. Google Search returned a 3.35% click-through rate against a 3.17% platform benchmark, holding above the line while running a substantial budget, and Microsoft Ads added 692 clicks from 32,643 impressions at 2.12%. Alongside the media, the site received the consent-mode rebuild: tracking that had been installed directly in the theme was removed, a fresh Google Tag Manager container was published, and the analytics and Microsoft tags were each gated behind the correct consent signal, then tested three ways before legal rescanned. The blog templates were tidied in the same stretch, with author names and links stripped from every post, the outdated header image replaced on the Sinclair and Rush acquisition post and across the All Posts template, and stray placeholder text removed. The WordPress database was updated after a platform release. None of that shows up in a metric, but it is the difference between a site that merely works and one that will keep working.
