MOXIE's Analysis

Precision was the story for Ranson and Associates in August. The Home and Auto search campaign worked a deliberately tight budget and turned 445 impressions into 34 clicks, a 7.64% click-through rate that runs at more than twice the 3.17% platform benchmark. That ratio matters more than raw volume here, because it means nearly one in thirteen people who saw the ad were far enough along to act on it. Geofencing did the complementary job, putting the agency in front of 15,829 impressions across the neighborhoods and businesses that make up the local book, building the kind of familiarity that pays off later when a policy comes up for renewal. The two work together rather than competing: search catches people already shopping, geofencing keeps the name in front of everyone else. For an independent agency in a category where the national brands outspend everyone, that combination of sharp intent capture and steady local presence is exactly the right shape. August held that shape without drifting.
