MOXIE's Analysis

Families shopping for insurance rarely act the moment they first think about it; they compare, they wait, and they come back, which makes consistent visibility across that long window the real job of the program. June kept Ranson & Associates in front of those households, with Google Search capturing high-intent prospects at an 8.11 percent click-through rate and GeoFencing layering local awareness across more than 16,000 impressions in the community. The search side did the precision work, meeting people the moment they typed in what they were looking for, while geofencing kept the agency's name circulating among nearby households who are not actively searching yet. When the geofencing audience ran a little thin against the available parcels, the team moved to source additional address lists and widen the pool so the campaign paces the way it should. Each channel had a clear role, and the month kept both pointed at it without asking either to do the other's job. What earns trust over time is exactly this kind of steady, honest execution, month after month. June kept the program's strengths in motion and set up July to keep sharpening the edges where the data shows room.
