MOXIE's Analysis

Insurance is a slow-burn decision, so the win each month is staying visible across the long stretch between a first thought and a signed policy. July kept Ranson & Associates in exactly that position. The Home and Auto search campaign did the precision work, meeting families the moment they typed in what they needed and converting at an 11.85% click-through rate that ran well past the 3.17% platform benchmark. That kind of efficiency on a lean budget is the mark of a campaign reaching the right households at the right time. GeoFencing carried the awareness side, layering the agency's name across more than 12,700 impressions among nearby families who are not actively shopping yet but soon will be. The two channels split the work cleanly, one catching high intent and the other planting familiarity, and neither was asked to do the other's job. Consistency like this is what quietly compounds into trust and referrals as the months add up. July kept both engines running true and carried the program's momentum into the heart of the shopping season.
