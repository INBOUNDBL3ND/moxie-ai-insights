MOXIE's Analysis

June handed Auctus a small but meaningful win: turning a partner's new book into a revenue stream the website actually works for. When Hitesh asked how best to market Ken Greenberg's Leadership: A Short Manual to Success, the answer was about visibility and conversion, not just a link buried somewhere. We mapped out three placements and, once the client gave the go-ahead, put all three live before month's end. A feature on the homepage puts the book in front of nearly every visitor; a dedicated /leadership-book page does the actual selling with the cover, overview, and both buy buttons; and a note on Ken's bio page lends credibility and an easy path through to the purchase. Website hosting and maintenance kept everything running smoothly underneath. It is the kind of responsive, get-it-done month that makes the site a more useful asset than it was thirty days ago, and it leaves a clean template for featuring future partner content the same way.
