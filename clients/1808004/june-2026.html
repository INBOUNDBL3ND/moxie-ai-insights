The throughline of June was demand capture, with search working to meet engineers and buyers at the exact moment they go looking for the pumps and parts the company sells. The team treated June as another round of steady delivery rather than a one-month spike. July picks up with budgets pacing well and the funnel primed to keep turning that intent into inquiries.
