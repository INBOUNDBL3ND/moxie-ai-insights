MOXIE's Analysis

July put the technical craft behind the Paraben program on full display, with the team resolving a site-wide issue that had quietly broken every embedded video on paraben.com. Rather than patching symptoms, the team traced the failure to its root: a security plugin's content-security policy was blocking the video players outright. With the cause pinned down, the policy was updated to allow the trusted video domains, and the team then verified playback restored across the demo and test pages so nothing was left to chance. For a company selling forensic and AI-driven platforms to specialized, technical buyers, working product demonstrations are not a nice-to-have; they are central to how the site earns credibility, so getting them working again directly protects the ability to convert serious prospects. That fix sat alongside the ongoing AEO and SEO work that keeps the site precisely optimized and answer-engine ready. It was a month of careful, foundational problem-solving, the kind that keeps a well-built site performing exactly as it should for the audience it is meant to reach.
