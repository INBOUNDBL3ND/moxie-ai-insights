MOXIE's Analysis

The throughline of June was the storefront and the showroom pulling in the same direction, with a new referral push on the website while search and video kept fresh shoppers arriving. On the site, the team updated the Referral page for Patriot's June Referral Bonus campaign, building in the campaign details from the flyer Trish sent so customers have an easy online way to send business Patriot's way instead of passing paper forms back and forth. On the paid side, Google Search met homeowners actively shopping the work, drawing a 6.27% click-through rate from people already looking, and a YouTube flight put the brand in front of roughly ninety-four thousand local screens to keep the showroom top of mind. The two layers reinforce each other: the ads bring the traffic, and a clean referral path gives existing customers a reason to act. There was also an opening conversation about handling the marketing for both of Trish's companies going forward, a sign the relationship is deepening. June was a busy, hands-on month with the referral campaign taking shape. The team heads into July ready to hold the paid momentum and pick that broader marketing conversation back up.
