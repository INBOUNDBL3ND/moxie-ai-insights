June became a pause point for Fuzzywumpets, and the most useful thing we can do is be straight about it. Early in the month we kept the everyday rhythm going, with organic social to hold the brand's visibility, ongoing SEO, and website upkeep, while also working to restore Facebook page access after the page was disconnected on the platform's side. Midway through June, the client made the call to pause all marketing services, and we honored that cleanly rather than stretching activity to fill the calendar. A pause is not a loss; it is a chance to let the account rest in a tidy state so that picking things back up is fast and painless whenever Fuzzywumpets is ready. Everything is documented, access is noted, and nothing is left half-finished. When the brand decides to re-engage, the runway is clear: the recurring services can switch back on without a scramble, and the next chapter starts from a clean, organized foundation rather than a mess to untangle.
