MOXIE's Analysis

Scale was the defining feature of Whitewater Freight's August. The LinkedIn program delivered 450,845 impressions and reached 432,379 people, an enormous share of the addressable audience for a freight brokerage, and it did so while the landing page was moved to the dedicated freight shipping services page so arriving traffic meets a focused offer rather than a general homepage. That change matters more than it sounds: when reach is this large, the quality of what happens after the click determines whether the spend compounds. Organic social ran on the same scale, drawing 431,183 impressions across the month and keeping the company visible to shippers and logistics managers between campaign flights. The two reinforce each other, with paid establishing the reach and organic making the brand look like a going concern when someone checks the profile. For a category where trust is earned before a single load moves, being this visible to this many of the right accounts is the groundwork worth laying.
