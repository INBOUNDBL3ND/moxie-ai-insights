MOXIE's Analysis

The commercial trash survey wrapped in early August and turned into something the City could actually govern with. Seven hundred and sixty-seven businesses were invited, forty-four completed responses came back, and the findings were unambiguous: ninety-eight percent said yes or maybe to a City-coordinated hauler, seventy-seven percent did not know about the six-thirty collection ordinance, and monthly costs ranged from fifty dollars to twenty-five hundred across at least five haulers. The team built a branded presentation with a graph for every question plus clear takeaways, ready for the August nineteenth Council meeting, with the raw exports available alongside. Elsewhere the business directory was rebuilt in Mailchimp from a fresh export so old records stop receiving mail, the fall Trailblazer articles were delivered, the 2026 BBA document was resent when the upload rendered badly, and when a concert post carried the wrong band photo it was corrected the same afternoon. Social reached 44,121 video views. Research, publishing and responsiveness all in one month.
