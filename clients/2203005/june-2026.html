MOXIE's Analysis

Residents planning their summer around Ellisville's calendar and the local businesses that anchor its main streets both turn to the city's channels to know what is happening, and June leaned right into that civic rhythm. Much of the month was responsiveness: the team replaced the dated Twitter logo on the website with current X branding, posted the Pop and Sons Dirty Soda ribbon cutting the day it happened, and added the board and commission volunteer form online with the corrected three o'clock EDC start time. On the design side, the team built a cover image and profile photo for the new Ellisville MO Business Group on Facebook, giving local shops a branded place to share ribbon cuttings and sales. The team also worked through delegate access so the city's X account can be managed cleanly going forward. The regular newsletter kept residents and the business community connected on a direct-to-inbox cadence, while social posts and website upkeep ran on their usual schedule. Ada also shared that she'd value more proactive, periodic website reviews, and the team is folding that into the cadence so aging elements get caught and flagged before they need to be reported. Into July, the same steady rhythm continues, with each piece reinforcing the others as one coherent city voice.
