Recognition season arrived on August twentieth when the Best Lawyers embargo lifted, and Jones Family Law Group was ready for it. Rather than bolt an announcement onto the site, the team audited all four attorney biographies against the new listings. Most credential entries were already written as an open range and needed nothing, which is the payoff from having structured them that way. A follow-up went to the firm about the 2027 badge files so those can be swapped in when they arrive. The site recorded 727 sessions. Credentials only work when they are precise, and August made them precise.
