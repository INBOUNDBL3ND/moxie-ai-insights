MOXIE's Analysis

June was a month of strengthening the foundation that every search, store, and ad campaign depends on. When the legal team flagged that tracking was firing before visitors made a cookie choice, the team rebuilt consent on the VisiPak site end to end: the CookieYes banner now offers a clear choice, Google Tag Manager holds all tracking until a visitor opts in, and a stray analytics install was switched off so everything flows through one consent-controlled path. Testing in a clean browser confirmed the site behaves exactly the way the lawyers asked. Alongside it, the team rebuilt lead tracking in GA4 so the right form completions register as conversions, verified the Search Console domain properties across VisiPak, Indepak, and Clamshell, and cleared a round of legacy 404s and a redirect loop so pages resolve cleanly. An old CNAME record left behind by a prior agency was traced and removed, tidying the DNS. The new VisiLock product video reached its approved cut and is set to anchor a dedicated page on the VisiPak site. None of this shows up in a headline metric, but it is the credibility a procurement buyer feels before they ever fill out a form. June leaves the whole multi-site presence cleaner and more compliant, and the next stretch is about putting that footing to work.
