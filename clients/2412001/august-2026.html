MOXIE's Analysis

August was a compliance month for Sinclair and Rush, and the team treated it that way. Consent-mode tracking was rebuilt across Visipak, Indepak and Clamshell Packaging so that no analytics or advertising tag fires before a visitor makes a cookie choice. That meant finding tracking installed directly in Divi theme options and in Rank Math, removing it, standing up fresh Google Tag Manager containers, wiring the consent platform in, and gating each tag behind the correct consent signal. Every site was then tested three ways, no action, reject all and accept all, and confirmed to behave correctly before legal was asked to rerun its scan. Two client meetings covered the work and the road ahead, including the account structure for OpenAI advertising, which will run one Sinclair and Rush billing account across per-brand advertiser accounts. LinkedIn continued reaching decision-makers in packaging, and the client agreed to export CRM contacts to widen the matched audience. Getting the tracking foundation legally clean before scaling anything on top of it was the right order to do this in.
