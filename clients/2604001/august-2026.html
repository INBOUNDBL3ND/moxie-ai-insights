MOXIE's Analysis

Reach on this scale is unusual, and the National Museum of Transportation earned it in August. Organic social drew 124,936 video views across the month alongside 229 likes and 271 engagements, numbers that tell you the content is being watched rather than merely served. That is the right measure for a museum, because the product is fundamentally visual: locomotives, aircraft and vehicles that photograph and film beautifully and that people genuinely want to look at even when they are not planning a visit. Turning that raw material into a steady stream of posts is what converts a collection into an audience, and an audience into weekend attendance. What makes the month notable is the ratio. Six figures of video views from organic posting alone, without anything behind it, means the material is doing the work and the calendar is putting it in front of people consistently. That is a rare position to be in, and August strengthened it further.
