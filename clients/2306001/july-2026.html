MOXIE's Analysis

July brought fresh energy to Elite Testosterone's local push, with Facebook advertising back up and running and a new billboard location added to the mix. On Facebook, the campaign reached more than 5,200 people across the surrounding communities and drew 114 clicks from households weighing a next step toward better health. The digital billboards kept the clinic's name visible along the busy corridors people drive every day, logging 26,700 impressions and reinforcing the message between screens. SEO continued underneath it all, strengthening how the practice surfaces when patients search on their own. Much of the month went into building for the new location: the team produced a deep creative package spanning homepage mockups, social posts, billboard art, Reels, and animated explainer scripts, then walked Duane through it in a July 16 meeting with a recap and a clear to-do list, loading every asset into the client portal. The business address was refreshed across the website, Facebook, and the Google Business Profile so patients find the right place from day one. Every channel had its own part to play, and July pointed all of them at the same goal: filling the new location with the patients Elite most wants to reach.
