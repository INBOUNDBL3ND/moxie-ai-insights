MOXIE's Analysis

Creative refresh drove the month for Elite Testosterone. A routine check of the Blip account turned up billboard artwork that was approved on some boards and rejected on others, so rather than let placements sit idle the team briefed an entirely new direction. Five landscape sizes were produced to cover the main billboard formats, built around the line Feel like you again, kept to the black, orange and white palette with the logo prominent and a single clear call to action. The new artwork was designed, approved and loaded into Blip before month end. On the advertising side, Facebook reached 7,424 people and delivered 22,082 impressions and 180 clicks across the service area, while the billboard ran 2,860 impressions and 88 blips on one board. The site recorded 300 sessions. Fixing a creative problem before it becomes an empty schedule is unglamorous work, and it is the reason September opens with approved artwork already in rotation rather than a queue of disapprovals to untangle.
