MOXIE's Analysis

June's paid mix did its job on two fronts at once: Facebook put the studio in front of more than 11,000 local people and pulled 571 clicks, while a lean Google Search campaign converted intent at an 18.6 percent click rate, one of the strongest the account has seen. Together that is nearly 15,000 impressions and just under 700 clicks pointed at women in the Ballwin and West County area looking for a reason to walk in. The team's main project for the month was sharpening how the studio measures results: a new Meta conversion built around the actual intro-offer purchase, confirmed live with a real booking and firing cleanly through both the browser pixel and server-side tracking, with the ad set rebuilt to optimize toward that purchase. What makes the paid combination effective is the division of labor, one channel building the familiarity that makes a brand feel like the obvious choice and the other catching the moment someone decides to act. With that cleaner tracking in place, the team handed the advertising off to the studio's coaching group in a smooth, well-documented transition, leaving the campaigns measuring the right thing for whoever runs them next.
