MOXIE's Analysis

July was a build month with a payoff on both ends: a brand-new website went live while the ads kept the phones ringing. On the paid side, Google P-Max turned 19,900 impressions into 796 clicks at a 3.99% click-through that edged past the 3.17% platform benchmark, keeping the company in front of homeowners the moment a mole problem starts. The bigger lift was the new site itself. The team designed and launched a fresh Divi WordPress build at whackamolestl.com, walked the client through it, and set up the MarkUp feedback tool and client portal so review stays easy. DNS moved from Wix to GoDaddy hosting so the site went live cleanly on both mobile and desktop, and a custom twelve-level branded mobile game with a lead-capture screen shipped live to give visitors a reason to stick around and raise a hand. Promo videos, QR codes, and refreshed imagery rounded out the package. With a stronger foundation now carrying the SEO and a search campaign clearly finding the right neighbors, the brand is set up for a busy stretch. July put the pieces in place to turn peak season into booked jobs.
