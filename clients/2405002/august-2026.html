MOXIE's Analysis

The new site went live on August 12, and the month around it was spent making sure it launched properly rather than merely launching. GA4 tracking went on and was verified in real time across the homepage, contact and FAQ pages. Rank Math SEO and the Pro add-on were installed and configured with the XML sitemap and the analytics connection wired in. An llms.txt file was written from the live site content so AI assistants answering questions about mole removal in St. Louis pull accurate, verifiable information rather than guessing, covering the business summary, the three treatment plans, the service area and five FAQ entries. When the client raised a 100% catch rate claim, the team pushed back and asked how it was measured before putting it on the page, which is the right instinct on a claim a competitor could challenge. Performance Max ran alongside all of this and delivered 215 clicks from 7,290 impressions. The site closed August with 338 sessions on a foundation that will hold up as the traffic grows.
