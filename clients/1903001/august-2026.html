MOXIE's Analysis

Email carried the relationship for Captifi Wealth Solutions this month, doing the job it is best suited to. The August newsletter went to 142 contacts, a list that is deliberately small and genuinely qualified rather than padded, and it opened at a rate that comfortably clears what most financial services senders see. That is the shape this channel should take for a practice built on trust: fewer people, all of whom actually chose to hear from the firm, receiving something worth opening. In a category where the decision to move a portfolio happens slowly and often years after the first conversation, staying gently and usefully present matters more than volume ever will. The newsletter does that without demanding anything, which is precisely why it works. August kept the cadence intact and the list engaged, and it left the practice a little more familiar to every person on it than it was at the start of the month. That accumulation is the return.
