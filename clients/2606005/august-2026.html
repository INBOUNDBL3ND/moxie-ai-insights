MOXIE's Analysis

The site went live on August eleventh, and getting there took some untangling. The original build sat with a company the owner had lost contact with, and the domain turned out to be registered somewhere different again, so the first job was tracing where everything actually lived and securing proper access. Once that was in hand the migration ran carefully: records were checked so nothing tied to email would break, domain management was moved across, the nameservers were reset, the main record was repointed at the new site and the www address was verified. The previous platform was deliberately left in place as a fallback until the new site was confirmed working, and it was checked across desktop and mobile once the certificate finished issuing. The business now owns a site it actually controls, on a foundation that can be updated whenever it needs to be.
