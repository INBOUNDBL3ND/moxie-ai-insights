MOXIE's Analysis

A practice in its first months has to build credibility and correct the record at the same time, and August did both. When the transfer to the Colorado chapter of the Academy was denied, the About Me page was rewritten to the client's approved copy the same week, the fellowship card was removed, the three remaining credentials were reflowed into a clean layout rather than left visibly short one item, and the accurate history of the prior Virginia membership was kept. Later in the month a sharper version of the same problem surfaced: the present-tense fellowship claim was still living in the search metadata, the social tags, the structured data and the llms file, where AI assistants were repeating it. Every instance was found and removed, the cache was cleared and the live pages were verified, which matters here because a lawyer's public claims about credentials are an ethics question, not a presentation one. Alongside that, a Google Business Profile was built, submitted and verified, office photos added, reels delivered and a first post set drafted.
