MOXIE's Analysis

July was launch month for Duer's Rackhouse, and the work spanned everything it takes to open a new outdoor store to the world. The team purchased the domain, stood up business email, and built the new website from the ground up around the brand's logo and direction, then carried it through review and took it live on desktop and mobile. Alongside the site, a full Facebook business page went up with branding, the outdoor-equipment category, and a secured username, ready to greet the community heading into the grand opening. The Facebook ads did real work in the meantime, reaching more than 21,000 people across better than 40,000 impressions and pulling a 7.57% click-through that ran far ahead of the 0.90% platform benchmark, a sign the message is landing with the hunters and outdoor families the store is built for. Four fresh campaigns went live to carry the grand-opening announcement into feeds, Stories, and Reels. For a store opening its doors, July did the most important thing of all: it gave Duer's Rackhouse a front door online that the right people can already find.
