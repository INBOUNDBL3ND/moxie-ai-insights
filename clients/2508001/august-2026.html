MOXIE's Analysis

Month two after launch, and the numbers say the audience found Duer's Rackhouse fast. Facebook reached 15,457 people across the area and pulled 1,562 clicks from 30,454 impressions, a 5.13% click-through rate that runs at more than five times the 0.90% platform benchmark. Rates like that do not come from budget; they come from a brand people are genuinely curious about and creative that gets out of the way. With inventory set to expand week over week, the conversation this month turned to where else that curiosity could be met, and a Google Business Profile went onto the plan so anyone searching the name or the category locally lands on something complete and current. Website management and organic social carried the rest, keeping the site accurate as the shelves fill. The interesting thing about a launch that performs this well is that the constraint shifts quickly from attention to readiness. August was about making sure the second half of that equation keeps pace.
