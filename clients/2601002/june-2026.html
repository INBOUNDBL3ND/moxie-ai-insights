MOXIE's Analysis

The partnership with Jeff Baker & Sons reached a real inflection point in June, the month the program added its first layer of paid reach. Early on, the team sorted out account access, set up a fresh Facebook ad account, and linked billing so everything could run cleanly. By mid-month the Meta ads were live, built around the three core offers, fifty off mulch delivery and installation, fifty off a shrub trim or leaf removal job, and a hundred off patio and hardscaping work, targeted tightly to the West and South County neighborhoods where Jeff already does his best work. Alongside that, the team stood up a Google Search test pointed at the same service areas and offers, sending neighbors straight to the most relevant pages on the site. The graphic design and social work that carried the brand all spring kept right on going underneath it all. From here, every click those ads send over is a household the brand gets a fresh chance to win, and the team will watch closely to see which offers earn the strongest response.
