MOXIE's Analysis

July was a month of getting the engine tuned and running clean for Jeff Baker & Sons. When the Google Search ads were not serving the way they should, the team dug in, traced the issue, and rebuilt a fresh Google Ads account with a simplified Search campaign that was confirmed live and working by July 23. To make sure every click gets measured from here on, GA4 was set up with a corrected measurement ID and verified collecting data. Facebook kept the brand in front of the community all month, reaching more than 5,500 nearby households and drawing 265 clicks to the offers, while the relaunched Search campaign began sending homeowners straight to the most relevant pages. Looking further ahead, the team built a set of WordPress mockups for a full SEO-focused site rebuild, covering All Services, Deck Wash and Stain, About, and Recruiting, and kicked off a graphic-design set with drive-by job-site yard signs and referral postcards. Every piece of this is about turning local attention into booked work. July cleared the technical hurdles and lined up a stronger foundation, so the back half of the season starts on solid ground.
