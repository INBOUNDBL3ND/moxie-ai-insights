A month of building things, then putting them in customers' hands. The new website went to Jeff for review at the start of August with a markup link and portal access, came back approved on the 26th, and was queued for launch by month end. In parallel the team produced four double-sided A-frame safety signs for mulch installation, paver patios, retaining walls and leaf removal, done in high-visibility neon yellow so they read from the street while keeping the branding front and centre, plus a postcard for the zero percent processing payment option. Google advertiser identity verification was submitted and accepted ahead of the September 2 deadline, which quietly protected the account from being limited. Facebook worked hard through all of it, returning a 1.27% click-through rate against the 0.90% platform benchmark and reaching 5,596 people across the Ballwin service radius with the carousel service offers, while search added 346 clicks. Organic social pulled 13,630 video views. Plenty got built this month, and all of it is now out working.
