MOXIE's Analysis

Summer is peak season for cooling calls, and July put every channel to work catching that demand. Google Search led the charge, pulling a 10.18% click-through that ran well past the 3.17% platform benchmark as homeowners across St. Louis and the Metro East typed in urgent AC needs. Google Display added real muscle too, landing a 2.27% click-through against a 0.5% benchmark and keeping the brand visible in the neighborhoods that book the most service. Facebook widened the net across nearly 89,000 impressions, reaching close to 8,800 people in the community, while website remarketing stayed close to the households that had already visited and nudged them back toward a booked call. Alongside the ads, the team handled the details that keep trust intact: re-ordering 250 more review cards at the client's request, sharing the June analytics recap, and running down a concern that Google reviews were not showing by confirming they render cleanly on Chrome, Safari, and Firefox. Paid attention and a well-tended reputation feed the same result, and July kept both moving in the right direction as the busy stretch rolls on.
