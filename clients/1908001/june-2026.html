MOXIE's Analysis

Two jobs ran in parallel through June: keeping the advertising engine running and sharpening the organic foundation underneath it. On the SEO side, the team worked through on-page optimization across the site, refreshing target keywords, meta descriptions, and title tags, and shifting the seasonal emphasis toward AC Repair to match what St. Louis and Metro East homeowners are actually searching for in the summer heat. They also published llms.txt and robots.txt files so AI answer engines and search crawlers read the site cleanly. On the operations side, the team stood up email and Jobber access for two new Inman Air hires and pulled the May marketing and Google Ads invoices so billing stayed clean and current. Across the paid channels, search continued to do the heavy lifting, pulling in homeowners actively typing in cooling and heating needs, while Facebook and display kept the brand in front of the neighborhoods that matter and remarketing stayed close to the people who had already visited. The ads create the demand and the site has to be ready to catch it. June set both sides up to keep converting attention into booked calls as the summer rush builds.
