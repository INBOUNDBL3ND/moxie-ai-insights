MOXIE's Analysis

August put a deadline on the calendar and the team hit it. When a news article about Bart began circulating and a television commercial was set to air the following Monday, a banner went live on the site pointing straight to the piece, in place before the spot ran. The advertising side matched that urgency. Google Search converted a serious budget into 3,800 clicks at a 12.48% click-through rate, nearly four times the 3.17% platform benchmark, which is the sort of number that only happens when the keyword set and the service pages are pulling in the same direction. Google Display added 1,470 clicks at 2.82%, well past the 0.50% benchmark for that format, while Facebook reached more than 108,000 people across the service area and website remarketing brought prior visitors back for a second look. The site recorded 6,860 sessions on the month. Every channel had a distinct job and each one held its lane, which is what let the team drop everything and turn a timely news moment around in a weekend.
