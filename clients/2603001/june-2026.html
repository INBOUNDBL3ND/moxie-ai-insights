MOXIE's Analysis

The work with Ben in June was a study in protecting what is already running while quietly tidying the edges. When Ben asked whether the old Bluehost account could finally be cancelled, the team dug into the setup before anything got switched off, and that caution paid off. It turned out the domain's DNS and email were still being served from that account, and the record pointing bolandenergy.com at the live GoDaddy site lived there too, so cancelling outright would have risked taking the site and contact-form email dark. The team mapped exactly where every piece lives, the website on GoDaddy, DNS and mailboxes still on JustHost, the domain registered at Bluehost, and laid out a clear keep-versus-cancel list. Only the unnecessary domain-privacy add-on was flagged as safe to drop, with everything else held in place until the move can be done without risk. Nothing was left to chance, and the program heads into July on a cleaner, better-documented footing than it started the month with.
