MOXIE's Analysis

Consistency did the work for Partners In Medicine in August. Organic social drew 969 video views and 45 likes across the month, keeping the practice familiar to the people in its area who may not need care today but will remember the name when they do. New posts and reels moved through production for the month ahead, so the calendar stays a month in front rather than being filled the week it is needed. Website management held the service and provider pages accurate underneath, and SEO kept them matched to how patients actually phrase what they are looking for, which is rarely the clinical term. The site recorded 698 sessions. What makes this work in healthcare is that the buying moment is unpredictable and the research window is short: someone decides they need a physician on a Tuesday and picks one by Thursday. Being already visible, already credible and already accurate on the day that happens is the entire game, and August held all three.
